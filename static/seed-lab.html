Why it never happens

A 12-word seed is one of 2128 ≈ 3.4 × 1038 possibilities. Even if a million wallets on the whole network held funds, the chance that one random guess lands on any of them is about 1 in 3.4 × 1032. Here is how long you would expect to wait to hit even one:

The takeaway

  • Random guessing never works. The keyspace is so vast that every computer on Earth, running for the whole age of the universe, would still check essentially none of it.
  • Addresses are public; that's fine. Security doesn't come from hiding the address — it comes from nobody being able to guess the seed behind it.
  • So how are wallets actually lost? Never by guessing. Only when a seed phrase is leaked, phished, screenshotted, or stored somewhere insecure. Guard the 12 words; that's the whole game.

Educational demo. Wallets are generated with the real Bittensor library and their balances read from the live chain; the seeds are thrown away. It cannot find anyone's funds — that's the point.
